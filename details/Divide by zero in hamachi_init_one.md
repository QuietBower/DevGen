# 🐛 Divide by zero in hamachi_init_one

## 📌 Overview
* **Location:** `drivers/net/ethernet/packetengines/hamachi.c`
* **Current Status:** 👀 **Confirmed**
* **Notes:** Caused by a flawed ternary operator check on the PCIClkMeas register.
